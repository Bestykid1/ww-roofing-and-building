# Local search launch checklist (Google Business Profile + Search Console)

The website and the Google Business Profile work together: the site convinces
people, the profile puts you in the map results where "roofers leeds" searches
actually land. These actions need the business owner (they verify ownership);
all content is prepared and listed here.

## 1. Google Business Profile (do first)

Create or claim at https://business.google.com with these exact details:

- **Business name:** WW Roofing & Building
  (exactly this - no "Leeds", no "Ltd", no keywords added; Google suspends
  profiles for name stuffing)
- **Primary category:** Roofing contractor
- **Secondary category:** Construction company
- **Service area business:** hide the street address, set service areas:
  Leeds, Crossgates, Garforth, Wetherby, Roundhay, Halton, Seacroft, Colton,
  Kippax, Chapel Allerton, Alwoodley, Horsforth, Cookridge, Adel, Shadwell, Selby
- **Phone:** [same number as the website]
- **Website:** [the domain once live]
- **Opening hours:** [to confirm]
- **Description (drafted, paste in):**
  "Roofing and building contractors based in Crossgates, east Leeds. Roof
  repairs, full re-roofs, slating, tiling, Velux windows and leadwork, plus
  extensions and new builds. We work across Leeds and the surrounding areas
  for homeowners, commercial clients and main contractors."
- **Photos:** upload the same work photos as the website (we will export a
  consent-cleared set). Add new job photos monthly; profiles with fresh photos
  rank and convert better.

## 2. Reviews (start immediately after the profile exists)

- Get the profile's review link (Business Profile > Ask for reviews).
- After every completed job, send it to the customer by text or WhatsApp with
  one line: "If you were happy with the roof, a Google review helps us more
  than anything else."
- Never buy, trade or incentivise reviews; never write your own. A steady
  trickle of genuine ones beats a burst of suspicious ones.
- Reply briefly to every review, good or bad.

## 3. Google Search Console (once the domain is live)

- https://search.google.com/search-console - add the domain property, verify
  via the DNS record (we can add it to the domain settings).
- Submit the sitemap: https://[domain]/sitemap-index.xml
- After 1-2 weeks, check Indexing > Pages: the homepage, service pages and
  area pages should all show as indexed.

## 4. Bing Webmaster Tools (ten minutes, worth it)

- https://www.bing.com/webmasters - use "Import from Google Search Console".
- Confirm the sitemap imported.

## 5. Consistency rule (matters more than any trick)

Name, phone number and area description must be written identically on the
website, the Google profile, Facebook and anywhere else the business appears.
Update the Facebook page's contact details to match the site at launch.
