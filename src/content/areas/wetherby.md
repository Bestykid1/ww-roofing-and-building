## Slating country, within easy reach

Wetherby's older streets are stone buildings under slate roofs, and coverings like that reward careful work. That plays to the slating, leadwork and roof repair side of what we do: slipped or broken slates, failed flashings, chimney and abutment details, and the roofline joinery and guttering that go with them. Re-roofing, tiling and Velux windows are covered too when a job calls for them. The market town sits north-east of Leeds, a straightforward drive from our Crossgates base on the east side of the city, so travel adds nothing to the price of a job there.

## Slate, stone and conservation

Walk through Wetherby's centre and it is stone and slate: the older property is stone built, and the roofs on it are typically natural slate or, on some period buildings, stone slate laid in diminishing courses. With slate, the covering usually outlasts its fixings: when slates begin slipping one by one, the common cause is corroded nails rather than failed slate, and sound slates can often be salvaged and relaid. Stone slate is heavy and relies on substantial timbers, so repairs need matching stone and a careful eye on the structure beneath. Lead flashings, valleys and chimney details do much of the waterproofing on buildings like these and deserve the same standard of work as the covering itself. Part of central Wetherby sits within a conservation area, so on some buildings a like-for-like covering may be expected. The modern estates around the town are mostly concrete tiled, and repairs there are more routine.

## How a Wetherby quote works

We come out, get up to the roof and measure it. On slate and stone slate that survey matters: we want to know how the slates are fixed, what condition the lead is in and whether the timbers are carrying the load as they should. You then receive a written price itemising the work, the materials and the access. If the condition of battens or timbers can only be judged once slates are lifted, the price says so in plain terms. The visit is no obligation, and the decision afterwards is entirely yours.
