# WW Roofing & Building - Pre-launch checklist

Everything the website needs from you before it can go live, and what can wait.
Nothing on the site is invented: where a fact was missing we used a visible
placeholder, and the site cannot be deployed while any placeholder remains.

## Launch-blocking (the site cannot deploy without these)

1. **Phone number** for all call buttons. Also: does WhatsApp work on it?
2. **Enquiry email address** (form notifications will also go here).
3. **Domain name** (e.g. wwroofingandbuilding.co.uk) - or tell us and we register it.
4. **Exact legal identity** for the footer, privacy and terms pages: sole trader,
   partnership or limited company, and the precise name (the logo reads W&W; the
   Facebook page reads WW - we are using "WW Roofing & Building" in copy as agreed).
5. **Confirm Crossgates can be published** as the base area (currently shown on the
   About, Contact and Areas pages).
6. **The two photos showing your lads' faces** (scaffold team shot, church ladder
   shot): get their OK to appear on the website, or we exclude them at launch.
   They are the only photos with people in and are worth having.
7. **Sign off the local housing statements** on the four area pages (Crossgates,
   Garforth, Wetherby, Roundhay). Each page lists its claims in a comment at the
   bottom of its file; we will send them as a short list to tick or correct:
   - Crossgates: inter-war/post-war semis and terraces, original concrete tiles,
     Victorian terraces towards Halton.
   - Garforth: estates built 1960s-1990s plus newer development, concrete
     interlocking tile dominant, plenty of extension activity.
   - Wetherby: stone-built period property around the centre with slate/stone slate,
     conservation area in the centre, modern concrete-tiled estates at the edges.
   - Roundhay: substantial Victorian/Edwardian stock, natural (typically Welsh)
     slate, original leadwork, loft conversions common.
8. **Confirm the heritage project details**: the large stone building re-slate
   (photos with the spire and city skyline). Confirm it is fine to feature, and
   anything you can tell us about it makes the project page stronger.
9. **Free visits/quotes**: the Crossgates page says the visit and quote cost
   nothing. Confirm that is your standing offer or we soften it.

## Strongly recommended at launch (not blocking)

10. **Google Business Profile** - see LOCAL-SEARCH-CHECKLIST.md. The site alone
    cannot win the Google map results; the profile is how you appear there.
11. **Vector or flat version of the logo** (the file supplied is a 3D render that
    only works on dark backgrounds). A flat version sharpens the header, favicon
    and social cards.
12. **Opening hours** (for Google and the contact page).

## Whenever you have them (site improves automatically)

13. Camera-original photos of the jobs already on the site (WhatsApp compressed
    everything; originals would sharpen the big images).
14. New photos as jobs finish. Most wanted: finished roofs from the ground on a
    sunny day, close-ups of leadwork, any new-build work, before/after pairs.
15. Any real accreditations, memberships or insurance details, with numbers.
16. Actual guarantee terms you offer, if any.
17. Verified locations for photographed jobs (unlocks "our work in X" claims on
    area pages, which are currently disabled).
18. Real customer reviews: the site has hidden slots ready for them; they appear
    as soon as we add the first genuine one.
19. Five questions for the About page, answered in your own words: why the trade;
    who does the work; how a typical job runs; what work you turn down; what
    handover looks like. Ten minutes on the phone is enough.

## Copy rules we followed (so you know what is NOT on the site)

- No invented years in business, job counts, reviews, ratings, accreditations,
  guarantees or insurance claims.
- No named clients or job locations.
- No stock photography: every image is your own work.
